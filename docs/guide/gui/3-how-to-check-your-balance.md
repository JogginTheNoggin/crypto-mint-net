# How to check your balance

## Summary

**Wallet**, **Balance**, **Show balance** lists what you can spend,
mint by mint. The wallet counts only the mints it watches, so add the
mints you are paid in with **Watch mints**.

## Show your balance

1. Open **Wallet**.
2. In **Balance**, press **Show balance**.
3. Type the passphrase if it asks.

It shows the spendable units for each watched mint.

## Watch the mints you hold

Coins live in mints, and the wallet looks for yours only in the mints and
pools it watches.

1. In **Watch mints**, fill in **Mint serials** (mint numbers, separated
   by spaces or commas) or **Pools**.
2. Press **Watch**.

Claiming a mint watches it for you ([How to claim a mint](5-how-to-claim-a-mint.md)).

## Other ways to look

- **Addresses** lists every address the wallet has given out.
- On **Ledger**, **Find** an address to see its balance, what it holds
  and its history, linked to the entries that hold them
  ([How to browse the ledger](4-how-to-browse-the-ledger.md)). This works
  for any address, not only yours, and needs no passphrase.

In the terminal: [How to check your balance (headless)](../headless/3-how-to-check-your-balance.md).
